# The text is the map

A Wardley map is a plain-text `.wmap` (or `.owm`) file. Open its source beside the canvas with
**Open Source to the Side** in the editor title, and the diagram with **Open Diagram to the Side**
from the text.

Both stay in sync: what you type appears on the canvas, and what you draw is written into the
text, one undo step at a time, without touching the lines you did not change.
