# Learn the basics

## Reading a map

Every component sits on two axes:

- **Up and down** is the value chain: the higher a component sits, the more visible it is to your
  user.
- **Left and right** is evolution: components mature from genesis, to custom-built, to product,
  to commodity.

Together they show _what_ you depend on and _how mature_ each piece is.

## Editing a map

- **Add and connect** — pick a tool in the palette at the top (or press `C` for a component, `U`
  for a user, `N` for a note) and type its name. Select an element and press `A`, or use its
  context pad, to draw a dependency.
- **Move, rename, evolve** — drag a component to reposition it and double-click it to rename it.
  Dragging it left or right evolves it. Drag a label to move just the label.
- **Resize the map** — drag the plot's right or bottom edge or its corner, or choose **Map size…**
  in the menu.
- **Undo and redo** — `Ctrl/Cmd+Z` and `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`, the same history as the text file.
- **Zoom** — the zoom panel in the bottom-right corner, `Ctrl/Cmd+Plus` and `Ctrl/Cmd+Minus`,
  `Ctrl/Cmd+0` for 100 % and `Shift+1` to fit the map.
- **Export** — the menu in the top-left corner sets the map size and the X-axis labels, exports PNG
  or SVG pictures (also via **Wardley: Export as …** in the Command Palette) and lists every
  keyboard shortcut. Exports are pictures: keep the `.wmap` or `.owm` file as the editable source.

## Text and diagram side by side

Click **Open Source to the Side** in the editor title bar to see the text next to the diagram. Your
edits on either side show up on the other right away, and canvas edits keep your comments and
layout of the text. Lines the parser cannot read are listed in the **Problems** view.

New to the concept? Start with [Learn Wardley Mapping](https://learnwardleymapping.com/).
